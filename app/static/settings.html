Gemini

Optional.

Stored only in output/settings.json on this computer. In an agentic mode, text from your paper (and LaTeX excerpts around errors) is sent to Google's Gemini API. Requests are spaced a few seconds apart to stay within the free tier's per-minute limit.

If this model is busy or has used up its free requests, Paper Forge steps down to the next Flash model, then tries the others, until one answers. The job log names the model that answered.

About free Gemini keys

  • Google limits how many requests a free key can send per minute and per day. The limits differ by model and Google changes them from time to time. See the current limits.
  • When Google's servers are busy, free requests can be slow or refused, so a paper can take several minutes longer. Paper Forge tries other models, and if none answers, that step uses its built-in rules, so you still get your PDF.
  • Google's terms for free use let it use what you send to improve its products, and human reviewers may read it. For a paper that must stay confidential, use Off mode, Ollama, or a key with billing turned on. Google's terms.
